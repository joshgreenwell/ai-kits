# USG-019 environmental action recommendation evidence

Verified: 2026-09-15

This is the primary-source research record for [USG-019](../usage-tasks/usg-019-environmental-action-recommendations.md). The implementation and verification record lives with [USG-020](../usage-tasks/usg-020-environmental-impact-section.md). It records three personal-user destinations that had a current public purchase or support path on the verification date. No order, donation, cart addition, subscription, delivery, or retirement was initiated while researching them.

The recommendations are separate from the Observatory's footprint model. They do not establish carbon neutrality, prove that an estimated footprint occurred, or claim to reverse electricity use or water consumption at an unknown datacenter.

## Claim-state rules for the UI

Use these states literally:

- **Contribution:** money supports an organization's mission. It does not buy a quantified environmental unit unless the destination expressly says it does.
- **Purchase:** the user paid for a stated product or unit. A receipt or order confirmation proves the transaction, not that the environmental result has already happened.
- **Commitment:** the provider has promised future delivery under the order. A commitment is still not delivery.
- **Delivery:** the provider reports that the specified benefit has occurred and has been assigned to the buyer. Require the program's delivery note, certificate, or equivalent evidence.
- **Retirement:** a serial instrument is taken out of circulation so it cannot be sold or claimed again. Require the program or registry's retirement evidence; do not infer retirement from payment alone.

The quality screen used for carbon follows the current [DOE CDR Challenge criteria](https://www.energy.gov/hgeo/cdr-challenge): net-negative accounting, additionality, at least 100 years of isolation, rigorous measurement/monitoring/reporting/verification, independent validation, unique quantification, and community-benefit safeguards. DOE also distinguishes a pledge, a binding purchase, and supporting purchase/registry documentation. The Challenge page itself is still marked "coming soon" and is guidance, not a DOE endorsement of any destination below.

For water, [WRI's Volumetric Water Benefit Accounting 2.0](https://www.wri.org/research/volumetric-water-benefit-accounting-2-0) starts with the local catchment and shared water challenge, then asks practitioners to quantify, track, and communicate benefits without double counting. A gallon in one catchment is therefore not evidence that a gallon was restored where an unknown datacenter operated.

For electricity, [EPA defines one REC](https://www.epa.gov/green-power-markets/renewable-energy-certificates-recs) as the non-power attributes of one MWh of renewable generation delivered to a grid. EPA's [environmental-claims guidance](https://www.epa.gov/green-power-markets/environmental-claims) says ownership and retirement support renewable-electricity-use claims but do not convey a direct emissions reduction. A REC is not a carbon offset and does not route particular electrons to a datacenter.

## Selected destination 1: durable carbon removal

### Climeworks Technology focus carbon-removal portfolio

- **Direct destination:** [Climeworks standardized carbon-removal self-service](https://climeworks.com/actnow), selecting **Technology focus**.
- **Purpose:** purchase a future delivery of 100% technology-based carbon dioxide removal. Climeworks' [Technology focus product sheet](https://climeworks.com/faq/climeworks-solutions-product-sheet-technology-focus) lists direct air capture, enhanced rock weathering, bioenergy with carbon capture and storage, and possible other technology-based removal methods.
- **Unit:** metric tonne of CO2 removal. The public destination quoted **USD 500 per tonne** on 2026-09-15 and offered EUR, USD, CHF, and GBP display currencies.
- **Availability:** the destination described the portfolios as self-service for individuals and businesses of any size. Climeworks also documents [one-time purchases](https://climeworks.com/faq/can-i-do-a-one-time-payment-for-co2-removal) and [monthly or annual subscriptions](https://climeworks.com/faq/how-does-my-subscription-work). No eligibility-country list was found on the reviewed pages.
- **Geographic scope:** atmospheric carbon removal has a global climate purpose, but the supplier, project, and project geography for a Technology focus order are not fixed on the public pre-purchase page. Those details arrive with delivery.
- **Delivery timing:** the purchase page and product sheet say delivery occurs **within seven years following the year of purchase**. The buyer receives an order confirmation and invoice at purchase and a delivery note, including supplier information, after delivery.
- **Verification, evidence, and retirement basis:** Climeworks says every portfolio solution, supplier, and project is verified and/or certified under recognized standards such as Puro or Isometric. The delivery note links to the verifier/certifier and registry when applicable. As evidence that Climeworks can produce and retire durable DAC removals, the first-party [Puro registry entry for Climeworks' Orca plant](https://registry.puro.earth/projects/631817) showed independently audited issuances and retirements, with its geologically stored CORCs labeled **1000+ years**, on 2026-09-15. Puro's [Geologically Stored Carbon methodology](https://puro.earth/methodologies/geologically-stored-carbon/) requires at least 1,000 years of storage for that pathway. Climeworks says Puro-accredited DNV verifies Orca batches and that Puro CORCs are [issued in the public registry and retired when delivered to a customer](https://climeworks.com/press-release/climeworks-first-dac-company-certified-under-puro-standard).
- **Minimums and fees:** the public destination disclosed the per-tonne price but did not expose a minimum order or separate fee in the reviewed content. Recheck the checkout before displaying price or minimum information.
- **State at purchase:** **purchase plus future-delivery commitment**. The order confirmation records quantity ordered; it is not a removal or retirement certificate.
- **State after evidence arrives:** mark **delivered** only from the delivery note. Mark **retired** only when the allocated supplier/standard provides retirement evidence.

**UI-safe recommendation**

> Fund a future delivery of verified, technology-based carbon removal through Climeworks. Your order confirmation records what you purchased; delivery may take up to seven years after the purchase year, and supplier and registry details arrive with the delivery note.

**Uncertainty and guardrails**

- The public pre-purchase page does not identify the eventual method mix, supplier, project, country, standard, registry, or serial credits for an individual order.
- The portfolio is marketed as "durable only," but the reviewed pre-purchase material does not publish one minimum durability term that applies to every possible portfolio component. The Puro 1000+ evidence applies to the named Orca geological-storage project, not automatically to every Technology focus unit.
- Do not label an order "removed," "delivered," "retired," "offset," or "neutralized" when payment succeeds. Preserve the pending-delivery state until evidence arrives.
- Do not imply that buying the modeled number of tonnes proves equivalence to the user's actual operational emissions; the Observatory estimate has unknown datacenter, grid, hardware, and scope inputs.

## Selected destination 2: water stewardship with catchment context

### BEF Jordan River Water Restoration Certificate

- **Direct destination:** [BEF Household Store](https://store.b-e-f.org/household/), **Water Restoration Certificates (WRC)**.
- **Purpose:** purchase quantified support for restoring and maintaining flow in Utah's Jordan River to Farmington Bay of the Great Salt Lake. BEF's [project portfolio](https://www.b-e-f.org/bef-wrc-portfolio/) says the project uses Environmental Water Transactions involving year-round industrial and municipal water rights during severe Utah drought.
- **Unit:** **1 WRC = 1,000 gallons** of water improved or restored to benefit people and nature.
- **Availability:** the household listing had an active quantity and add-to-cart form on 2026-09-15, with one-time, annually renewing, and gift choices.
- **Geographic scope:** Jordan River to Farmington Bay of the Great Salt Lake, Utah, United States. This is the supported catchment; it is not presumed to be the catchment of any modeled AI water use.
- **Delivery timing:** the public listing did not state a project vintage, benefit-delivery date, or delivery SLA. BEF's [FAQ](https://www.b-e-f.org/faqs/) says the purchaser receives a certificate identifying the project, description, volume, registry, and vintage, but does not give a delivery deadline.
- **Verification, evidence, and retirement basis:** BEF says its WRC projects are third-party verified, tracked, and registered. The portfolio identifies **Watercourse Engineering** as the Jordan River project's verifier. BEF's store says its environmental products are retired on the buyer's behalf, and its FAQ describes the post-purchase certificate. The reviewed public material did not expose the Jordan River vintage, a registry record, serial number, or retirement record before purchase.
- **Minimums and fees:** the live listing showed **USD 4 per WRC** and a minimum quantity of **one WRC**. No separate fee was disclosed on the listing; recheck the checkout before displaying price or fees.
- **State at purchase:** **purchase** of a WRC and support for a named catchment project. Payment alone is not public evidence of benefit delivery.
- **State after evidence arrives:** use the issued certificate to record project, volume, registry, and vintage. Call it **retired** only if BEF's purchase evidence confirms retirement; the public landing-page statement alone is not a buyer-specific retirement receipt.

**UI-safe recommendation**

> Support 1,000 gallons of verified water restoration in Utah's Jordan River–Great Salt Lake catchment with one BEF Water Restoration Certificate. This supports that named catchment; it does not claim to reverse water use at the unknown datacenter behind this estimate.

**Uncertainty and guardrails**

- The public checkout did not disclose the WRC's vintage, benefit period, registry, serial identity, or buyer-specific retirement timing.
- "1,000 gallons" is the WRC product unit, not a finding that the user's modeled liters occurred in Utah or that the same local water impact was reversed.
- Keep the catchment name visible. Do not shorten this to "replace your water use," "water neutral," or an unqualified gallon-for-gallon reversal claim.

## Selected destination 3: cleaner-electricity and efficiency support

### Rewiring America contribution

- **Direct destination:** [Donate to Rewiring America](https://donate.rewiringamerica.org/campaign/641970/donate).
- **Purpose:** contribute to a U.S. nonprofit that develops tools, campaigns, partnerships, and community programs intended to make efficient electric home upgrades easier and more affordable. Its [program description](https://www.rewiringamerica.org/about-us) covers household planning tools, community support, training, financing, and market/policy work. Its March 2026 [program update](https://www.rewiringamerica.org/newsroom/press-releases/fast-company-list-most-innovative-second-year) reports 2025 work including more than 60 state-level contractor networks, electric-coach support, and community bulk-purchase pilots.
- **Unit:** U.S. dollars contributed. This is **not** a kWh, MWh, REC, offset, avoided-emissions unit, or guaranteed appliance installation.
- **Availability:** the official online donation form was live on 2026-09-15. It also offered check, donor-advised-fund, and wire-transfer support by contacting the organization.
- **Geographic scope:** homes, businesses, communities, and electricity-system work in the United States.
- **Delivery timing:** an email receipt is sent after a successful donation. The donation page does not promise a donor-specific project, installation, electricity saving, or completion date.
- **Verification, evidence, and retirement basis:** the contribution receipt verifies the donation amount and date. Rewiring America's first-party pages describe its mission and reported program activity, but the reviewed destination provides no donation-specific environmental allocation or independently verified electricity outcome. No certificate is purchased and retirement does not apply.
- **Minimums and fees:** no minimum contribution or donor fee was disclosed in the reviewed public page content. Confirm the form before displaying either.
- **State at contribution:** **contribution only**. It is not a purchase, environmental delivery, or retirement.
- **State later:** there is no donor-specific delivery or retirement state unless Rewiring America separately supplies one; do not manufacture one from organization-wide reporting.

**UI-safe recommendation**

> Support Rewiring America's U.S. work to make efficient electric home upgrades easier and more affordable. This is a contribution to ongoing programs, not a purchase of electricity, RECs, measured kWh savings, or cleaner power for the datacenter behind this estimate.

**Uncertainty and guardrails**

- The destination does not allocate an individual donation to a specific household, appliance, grid project, energy quantity, or completion date.
- Organization-reported program activity is not a donor-level causal or environmental claim.
- Keep this under **support cleaner electricity and efficiency**, not under carbon removal or renewable-electricity matching.

## Reduction action retained from the product direction

The recommendation set should still lead with reduction:

> If the workload mix stays comparable, 10% fewer model calls would reduce the displayed electricity, direct-water, and operational-carbon scenarios by 10%. This is a modeled scenario, not a measured saving; a different workload mix can change the result.

This wording preserves the existing method's linear per-call example without implying that every call has the same measured impact.
